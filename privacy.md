# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 7 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how **[APP NAME]** (the "Service") collects, uses, and protects information when you use it through Discord. The Service is operated by **[OPERATOR NAME]** ("we", "us", "our").

## 1. Information we collect

**From Discord, when you interact with the Service:**
- your Discord user ID, username, and display name;
- the server (guild) ID, channel ID, and message ID of the interaction;
- the content of the messages, commands, attachments, or images you send to the Service;
- your locale and any roles relevant to permissions, where needed for the feature.

**Generated while using the Service:**
- model prompts and responses, and token/usage counts;
- error and diagnostic logs (timestamps, request IDs, latency, error codes);
- rate-limit and abuse-prevention records.

We do **not** request your email address, password, payment details, or Discord OAuth scopes beyond what a feature explicitly needs.

## 2. How we use information

- to generate responses and provide the features you request;
- to operate, maintain, debug, and secure the Service;
- to detect abuse, enforce our Terms, and comply with legal obligations;
- to measure aggregate usage and improve reliability.

We do **not** sell your personal data, and we do **not** use your message content to train models unless a specific feature says so and you opt in.

## 3. Legal bases

Where the GDPR or similar law applies, we rely on: performance of a contract (providing the Service), legitimate interests (security, abuse prevention, improving the Service), consent (where a feature asks for it), and legal obligation.

## 4. Sharing

We share data only with:

- **Discord**, as necessary to operate inside its platform;
- **infrastructure and hosting providers** that process data on our behalf under contractual confidentiality;
- **model inference providers**, if a feature uses a hosted model rather than a local one;
- authorities, where required by law or to protect rights and safety.

Service providers are bound to use data only to provide their service to us.

## 5. Retention

- Interaction content and model prompts/responses: kept for up to **30 days** for abuse prevention and debugging, then deleted or aggregated.
- Diagnostic and error logs: up to **90 days**.
- Abuse and moderation records: up to **12 months**.
- Data required for legal compliance: retained for the period the law requires.

Retention periods may be shorter where a feature does not store content at all.

## 6. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures, including access controls, encryption in transit, and least-privilege credentials. No method is perfectly secure; we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 7. Children

The Service is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age of digital consent in your country). We do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has provided data, contact us and we will delete it.
